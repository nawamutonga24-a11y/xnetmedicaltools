3. Ferriman‑Gallwey Parameters

The Ferriman–Gallwey score is a clinical tool used to quantify hirsutism (excess hair growth in women) by scoring hair distribution across specific body areas. It’s widely applied in endocrinology, especially for diagnosing PCOS and other androgen‑related disorders.
